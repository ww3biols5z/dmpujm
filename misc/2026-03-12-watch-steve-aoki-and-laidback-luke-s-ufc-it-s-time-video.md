# Watch Steve Aoki And Laidback Luke's UFC 'It's Time' Video

[And Laidback Luke's UFC](https://matrix-skin.xianfengchem.com/blog/0900945261.html)

Steve Aoki is well known for his (sometimes costly) commitment to fitness while touring the world, so it's no surprise that the DJ's latest music video is filled with hard work, running, and tons of sweat.

Add in Laidback Luke, a fellow DJ who happens to be a kung fu world champion, and UFC announcer Bruce Buffer, and you get an intense, fight-focused video that more than matches the intensity of the bumping EDM track. Check out the world premiere of the video for the song, "It's Time."

The two DJs trained for the video at the UFC Performance Institute, running through some of the drills they'd perform if they were actually training as part of a UFC fight camp. They hit bags, pull sleds, flip tires—and no, fight fans, your eyes aren't playing tricks on you: That is indeed UFC legend Ken Shamrock pummeling Aoki's abs and pushing him through the workouts. Aoki worked on some MMA-style jiu jitsu grappling, while Laidback Luke leaned on his strengths with kung fu kicks.

The video ends before the first punch is thrown, so fans will just have to imagine who would win the bout. If Aoki's allowed to make good on his nickname and throw a cake for his signature move, they might just call it a draw.
