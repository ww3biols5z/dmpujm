Uniqlo Men's Linen Long-Sleeve Shirt

There is something naturally flattering about wearing a linen shirt. Whether it’s the build-in relaxed wrinkles or the way it loosely skims the body, a great linen shirt can do wonders for any man’s figure. Score this Uniqlo French-linen shirt that’s a top-notch choice with tailoring that keeps it trim through the body and sleeves with just the right shirt length. For less than $20, this is one summer shirt worth trying that won’t break the bank.
