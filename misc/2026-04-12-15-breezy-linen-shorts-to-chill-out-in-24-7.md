What to Look for in Men's Linen Shorts

Fabric: The best linen shorts for men should be made from a high-quality linen fabric that is woven tightly to score high on durability. If you're looking at a linen-blend fabric, the higher the linen percentage is, the more breathable and linen-y it'll feel.

Fit: Men's linen shorts come in various cuts, such as slim-fit, regular-fit, or relaxed-fit. Choose a style that flatters your physique and aligns with your desired aesthetic. Additionally, pay attention to details like the rise, inseam length, and leg opening to ensure a well-proportioned look.

Waistband: More often than not, linen pants have an elastic waistband with either a drawstring or a button closure. Drawstring linen pants are often more casual and paired with a low-rise waist; on the other hand, buttoned-closure linen pants are your office-friendly slacks for dressing up since they often look more elevated than the drawstring ones.
