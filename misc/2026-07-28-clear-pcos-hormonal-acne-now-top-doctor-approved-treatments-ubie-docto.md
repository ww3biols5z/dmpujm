Hormonal acne, especially in individuals with Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS), can be effectively managed with certain treatments that target hormone levels.

1. Birth Control Pills: These are often used to manage hormonal acne. They work by regulating hormones that can cause acne, making them a common choice for women with PCOS.

2. Anti-Androgen Medications: These medications help reduce the levels of male hormones (androgens) that can lead to acne. By lowering these hormone levels, the treatment can help clear the skin.

According to the references, both birth control pills and anti-androgen medications are important in treating hormonal acne. They work by addressing the root cause, which is often related to a hormone imbalance in PCOS. If you're experiencing symptoms of hormonal acne or PCOS, you can check your symptoms with a free online assessment to better understand your condition. It is always important to talk to a healthcare provider to find the best treatment plan for individual needs.
