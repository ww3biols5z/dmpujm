Explanation

Sleep apnea is a condition where breathing stops and starts during sleep. This can lead to feeling very tired during the day, which is called daytime fatigue. People with sleep apnea often have trouble concentrating and remembering things.

How Sleep Apnea Affects Daytime Fatigue and Concentration:

Daytime Tiredness: When you have sleep apnea, your sleep is interrupted many times during the night. This means you don't get the deep sleep your body needs, making you feel tired when you're awake.

Trouble Focusing: Because your brain isn't getting enough rest, it can be hard to pay attention or remember things. This can make daily tasks more difficult.
