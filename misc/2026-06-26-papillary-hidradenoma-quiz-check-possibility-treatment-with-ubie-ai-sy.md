What is Papillary Hidradenoma?

This is a non-cancerous tumor found most commonly on the vulva, in the female genital area. It presents as a firm, skin-red colored bump that is slow-growing. It can ulcerate and cause symptoms. Exact cause is unknown.

Typical Symptoms of Papillary Hidradenoma

Bumps or swellings around the vulva/vagina

Skin lumps

Lump in the space between the genitals and anus (perineum)

Ulcers in the genital area

Hard or stiff skin

Areas of irritated or peeling skin

Skin problems

Diagnostic Questions for Papillary Hidradenoma

Your doctor may ask these questions to check for this disease:

Do you have any lumps or boils in your genital area?

Have you noticed any lumps on your skin?

Do you feel any lumps between the genital and anus?

Do you have any ulcers in your genital area?

Have you noticed any hard or stiff areas on your skin?

Treatment of Papillary Hidradenoma

Surgical removal of the papillary hidradenoma is the standard treatment. The prognosis is excellent.
