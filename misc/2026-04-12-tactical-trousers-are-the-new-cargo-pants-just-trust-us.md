What to Look For in Tactical Pants

Fabric: The best tactical pants can handle all sorts of wear and tear. In general, look for a pant with modern synthetic fabrics, typically involving polyester and cotton, for a durable build. There are some good options that are made with 100 percent cotton. Just make sure that it is a beefy heavyweight cotton that can take a beating.

In most cases, tactical pants should have some degree of water resistance and be quick drying. Some professions and uses may not require this attribute, but it is best to have in general. Look for any indicators of how fabrics perform with moisture before buying.

Fit: Oversized baggy cargo pants may be in fashion right now, but that look is not practical when it comes to tactical apparel. The best tactical pants are form fitting but not snug. This balanced fit will provide ample range of motion on the move while keeping any stowed away items from rocking around too much. Look for a straight leg or slim-straight fit to avoid too much loose fabric.

Pockets: Half of the job for tactical pants is to allow every item you may need in the field to be within reach. Not only do you want several more pockets than average pants, but they have to be strategically located for easy access.

Also, in most cases, it is best to have a higher number of smaller pockets than a couple really big pockets, because smaller pockets will keep items from bouncing around on the go.
