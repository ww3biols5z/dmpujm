What factors might be contributing to my gaining belly fat?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Belly fat gain is commonly caused by chronic stress, a diet high in processed foods, and the body's natural fat storage patterns—particularly in men, who tend to store excess fat around the abdomen due to hormonal factors. Other contributors include poor sleep, lack of physical activity, insulin resistance, and aging, which slows metabolism and shifts fat distribution.

Explanation

There are several reasons why you might be gaining belly fat.

Stress: Stress can cause your body to make more of a hormone called cortisol. When you have too much cortisol, your body might store more fat in your belly area. This is because cortisol can change how your body uses and stores fat.

[Gaining Belly Fat NOW](https://allpepay.com/blog/7781113433.html)

Processed Foods: Eating a lot of processed foods can also lead to more belly fat. Processed foods often have a lot of sugar, unhealthy fats, and calories, which can make it easy to gain weight, especially around your belly.

Fat Storage in Men: For men, it's common to store more fat around the belly. This is because of how men's bodies are designed to store fat.
