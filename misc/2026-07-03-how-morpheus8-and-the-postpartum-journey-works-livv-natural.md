What is postpartum?

It refers to a period after childbirth. In most women, this period lasts between four to six weeks.

It’s during this phase that you adjust emotionally and physically to your new role.

Some women can become sad and overly tired during the first few days. This condition lasts approximately three to four days. Many know it as ‘baby blues.’

When it continues for an extended period, it’s known as postpartum depression (PPD). Also called ‘postnatal depression.’

PPD, also a mental condition, affects one out of seven women. It’s believed that a hormone imbalance is the reason for this ailment.

During pregnancy, your hormones, like estrogen and progesterone, are extremely high.After giving birth, these hormone levels return to normal. The sudden drop can result in depression and anxiety.
