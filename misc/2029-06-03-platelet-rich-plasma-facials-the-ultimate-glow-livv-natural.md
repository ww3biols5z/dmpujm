What is a platelet-rich plasma facial?

The body’s ability to heal itself is truly amazing, and a platelet-rich plasma (PRP) facial is proof. Also known as the vampire facial, this beauty treatment may offer many benefits.

A platelet-rich plasma vampire facial uses a high concentration of platelets. These colorless cell fragments are developed in your bone marrow. They come from stem cells that form into platelets and white and red blood cells.

Studies reveal platelets could help with creating clots. They may also prevent and stop bleeding and regenerate the skin.

The vampire facial is used to stimulate the formation and release of active proteins. It also triggers growth factors, which promote healing and skin rejuvenation.

PRP facial vs. traditional facial

Both platelet-rich plasma and traditional facials are beneficial. However, the effects of PRP are incomparable.

Let’s unpack the differences between these two treatment options.

Traditional facial

A traditional facial serves as a rewarding skin care treat. It typically starts with a steam, which opens up your pores and frees sebum. Exfoliation is usually the next step to remove dead skin cells.

Typical facials also include face masks for skin hydration. They usually end with a massage, which helps you relax and removes excess fluids. You typically find this treatment option at a spa. You could also do a facial at home with a few basic products.

The biggest benefit of a traditional facial is that it’s incredibly relaxing. It also improves your mental state. The treatment stimulates your facial tissue. It brings more blood and nutrients to the surface. This effect helps even out your skin and prevents breakouts.

When done regularly, facials may delay the signs of aging. To get lasting effects out of traditional facials, you need to do them every 4–6 weeks.

A PRP facial is different from a traditional one. This treatment involves drawing your blood and injecting it into your skin. It’s a stark contrast from the massage and face mask that comes with typical facials.

What to expect from a PRP session

Before your PRP facial, the practitioner draws a small vial of blood from your arm. The total amount is about 10–15 milliliters (one tablespoon), so the process is typically quick.

Next, they spin it in a centrifuge. This machine rotates rapidly to separate the red blood cells from platelet-rich plasma.

Once the practitioner collects your PRP, they start the treatment. A specialized microneedling device causes tiny puncture wounds on the skin.

The wounds serve as microchannels that carry the PRP deep into the dermis. After microneedling, the physician gently applies the platelet-rich plasma to your skin. The facial can take around one and a half hours to complete.

Your skin may look bloody and swollen, but it’s not permanent. Redness, swelling, and itchiness typically lasts anywhere between 12–24 hours. There may also be some peeling in the days to come.

During aftercare, avoid wearing makeup. It could clog your pores and trigger breakouts. Results are usually visible after 2–3 weeks, but can take longer in some cases.
