Can a primary care physician diagnose and treat common sleep disorders?

Primary care physicians (PCPs) can diagnose and treat common sleep disorders, including insomnia, sleep apnea, and restless legs syndrome. PCPs typically begin with a detailed medical history, sleep diary review, and validated screening questionnaires. For suspected conditions like obstructive sleep apnea, they may order home sleep studies or refer patients to a sleep specialist for polysomnography. Treatment options a PCP can provide include lifestyle counseling, cognitive behavioral therapy for insomnia (CBT-I) referrals, and prescription medications when appropriate. Complex or treatment-resistant cases are typically referred to board-certified sleep medicine specialists.
