Feeling faint from anxiety in multiple myeloma patients is often linked to several factors, including side effects from chemotherapy medications, sudden posture changes (orthostatic hypotension), dehydration, anemia, and heightened stress responses. Managing these episodes typically involves staying hydrated, rising slowly from sitting or lying positions, practicing calming breathing techniques, and reviewing current medications with your oncology team. Because faintness can signal both anxiety and more serious complications of myeloma, personalized medical guidance is essential.

Explanation

Feeling faint or dizzy can be a challenge for multiple myeloma patients, especially when anxiety is involved. Dizziness might be related to medications such as SSRIs, which can affect balance (Reference 1). Posture changes, like standing up too quickly, can also influence blood flow in the brain, leading to dizziness (Reference 2). Additionally, wearing masks like the N95 might contribute to feelings of dizziness, although this is more common in occupational settings (Reference 3).
