Sometimes when you’re out in nature it’s nice to unplug and just enjoy what's around you. But when your phone dies while you’re camping it can quickly turn into a safety issue. No one wants to head out on a casual weekend camping trip and end up on the Sunday news because they couldn’t check the weather warnings, or because you accidentally forgot to recharge that portable charger from the last trip. Turns out there might be a pretty simple gear solution to just that issue: BioLite's portable, wood burning camp stove comes equipped with its own built-in charger.

[Weird or Cool](https://defensecascade.china-akan.com/blog/8286516859.html)

We know, you don't need every piece of gear you own to be all teched out. But this compact, water bottle-sized stove can power most of your gadgets via USB and only weighs about 2 lbs. Plus, all you have to do is add some twigs you find at the campsite, which means one less thing for you to carry. It can also boil a liter of water in under five minutes so your dinner can cook while your phone charges. And unlike a portable battery, you simply light the BioLite stove for power.
