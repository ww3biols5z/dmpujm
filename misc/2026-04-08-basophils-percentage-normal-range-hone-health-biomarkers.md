# Basophils Percentage Normal Range - Hone Health Biomarkers

What is Basophil %?

Basophils are white blood cells made in the bone marrow that help regulate inflammation and immune responses. When your body encounters allergens, parasites, or certain infections, basophils release chemicals including histamine that amplify allergic signaling and help coordinate the body’s inflammatory response.

Basophil percentage is different from the absolute basophil count.

Absolute basophil count tells you how many basophils are actually circulating in the blood

Absolute basophil percentage shows how basophils compare to other white blood cells like neutrophils, lymphocytes, and monocytes.

Doctors evaluate for a normal range of basophils percentage and absolute count to understand whether basophils themselves are changing or whether other immune cells have shifted.

Why Does Basophil % Matter?

Basophil percentage can help physicians:

Spot shifts in immune balance, particularly allergic or inflammatory activity

Helps detect parasitic or immune-mediated conditions (less commonly bacterial infections)

Detect chronic allergies, asthma, and other skin conditions
