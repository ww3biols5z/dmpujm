# How a Masters Champion Warms Up Before a Round of Golf

There is a conviction among the most ill-informed of trolls that golf is not a sport, and its participants aren’t athletes. Tiger Woods did his best to silence the masses, ushering in a new era of gym rat PGA Tour pros, but the reputation remains. One look at the statures of Rory McIlroy, Brooks Koepka, or Dustin Johnson, all top 10 golfers in the world, will quickly dispel this notion.

You need to work specific muscles to better your golf game, and it’s not always true that the bigger the better. The natural golf swing is less biceps, chest, and back, and more hips, core, and shoulders. The right movement patterns need to be reinforced, but unnecessary bulk can limit mobility.
