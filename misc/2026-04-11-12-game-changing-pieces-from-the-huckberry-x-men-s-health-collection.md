As the weather begins to transition with that first brisk fall breeze, it’s the perfect time to rethink your wardrobe. Forget what old duds live in the dark corners of your closet and take a moment envision the perfect collection of clothing that fits your everyday needs with enduring style. From your 9-to-5 grind in the city, to those much needed weekends of R&R in the great outdoors, the perfect pieces to add to your closet should have a sharp, classic look for all occasions and be made of technical fabrics that allow you to move with ease.

For the editors at Men’s Health, a garment must have the perfect balance of style and function to receive our seal of approval (both for features and our own personal closets). Huckberry—the online retailer for the stylish outdoorsman—shares this same belief, so naturally, it was only fitting that we collaborate on the best clothing a guy could ask for.

The Huckberry x Men’s Health capsule collection—featuring exclusive apparel created with the retailer’s in-house brand Proof—was designed with active guys on-the-go in mind. Each piece is made of innovative, breathable fabrics with functional features such as added stretch and moisture-wicking technology. For work, play, and travel, these timeless pieces will be a welcomed addition to your wardrobe this fall and for seasons to come.
