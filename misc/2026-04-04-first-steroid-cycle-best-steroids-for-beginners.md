First Off, What The Hell Are Steroids?

People are quick to talk about how steroids cause ‘roid rage’ and how they’re so dangerous.

Yet if you were to ask them what steroids are they would almost certainly draw a blank.

You see, there are many different types of steroids out there, and each one functions in a different way.

Some steroids just give you a little boost of energy.

Whereas others help to turn you into a muscle-bound freak.

There are steroids for fat loss, steroids for definition, steroids for recovery, steroids for muscle growth.

And steroids for cognitive function, and they still much better than Synthol or any kind of bodybuilding oils.

That still doesn’t answer the question of what they are.

Okay, well, anabolic steroids are basically synthetic versions of the hormone known as testosterone.

Testosterone is a dominant male sexual health hormone that is extremely anabolic.

This means that it plays an important role in muscle growth and repair.

Initially, they were discovered in the 30s, when scientists found that they increased lean muscle mass in animals.

More tests were conducted, and in the late 50s and early 60s, it was found that they could provide medical benefits for humans.

Some steroids were designed to assist with breathing problems.

Whereas others were designed to help promote brain health and function.

Or to promote healthy circulation and red blood cell formation.

Again, however, it was discovered that they also provided significant increases in a person’s overall muscle mass, size, strength, and athletic performance.

Once this was discovered, athletes and sportsmen and women started using these drugs for athletic purposes.

Remember, back then they weren’t banned.

And, in fact, many countries prescribed their national Olympic teams with these drugs to help give them an edge over their competitors.

By the 80s, however, more side-effects were discovered and the FDA began to clamp down on steroids until they were banned entirely and made illegally in underground labs.
