# Shawn Mendes Says He Used to Skip Sleep for 2-Hour Workouts

Shawn Mendes may be known for unrestrained vulnerability in his songs, but the singer is only now opening up about his once-harmful views of his body—and how he changed his perspective.

In a recent British GQ article released on Nov. 26, Mendes shared that he used to give up sleep to workout, having an unhealthy obsession over perfecting his physique. The 22-year-old pop star talked candidly about the unhealthy and dangerous habit, saying the pressure of being the "pinnacle of fitness" came from fearing if he wasn’t physically fit, he’d lose fans.

"Some days I would have three hours of sleep [because] I'd get up two hours early just to be able to work out,” Mendes told the magazine.
