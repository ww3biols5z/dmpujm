Can stress or anxiety make sleep myoclonus worse?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Can stress or anxiety cause sleep myoclonus? Current medical references do not directly link stress or anxiety to sleep myoclonus. However, because stress and anxiety are known to disrupt sleep quality and nervous system regulation, they may contribute to sleep disturbances that resemble or accompany myoclonic activity. If you're experiencing sudden muscle jerks during sleep along with stress or anxiety, it's worth investigating further to rule out underlying causes.
