Comparing In-Home vs. Clinic Treatments

When relieving stomach flu symptoms, you have two options: at-home treatment or a visit to a clinic. Both options can be effective, but there are some key differences to consider when making a decision.

At-home treatment usually involves rest, hydration, and over-the-counter medications such as ibuprofen and acetaminophen for fever and pain relief. This can be a good option if the symptoms are mild and you don’t want to wait in line at a clinic. However, if the symptoms are severe or don’t improve with time, you may need medical attention.

Visiting a clinic is often the best option if the stomach flu has become more serious. A doctor or nurse practitioner can assess your condition and provide treatment that may include IV fluids, antibiotics, anti-nausea medications, or other medications to ease your symptoms.

When Should a Person Visit a Doctor for Stomach Flu?

It is important to seek medical attention immediately when experiencing severe stomach flu symptoms. If you experience extreme abdominal pain, high fever, vomiting, diarrhea, or dehydration, it is important to visit a doctor or hospital immediately. You should also visit a doctor if your symptoms have lasted more than 48 hours without improving.
