What are the most common sleep disorders and how are they diagnosed?

Common sleep disorders include insomnia (difficulty falling or staying asleep), sleep apnea (breathing interruptions during sleep), and restless legs syndrome (uncomfortable leg sensations at night). Diagnosis typically involves a review of your medical history, overnight sleep studies (polysomnography), and validated questionnaires to assess symptom severity and patterns.
