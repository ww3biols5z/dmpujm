How does iron deficiency appear on a blood test for someone with multiple myeloma?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Iron deficiency in people with multiple myeloma typically appears on a blood test as low hemoglobin and low serum iron levels. Because multiple myeloma affects the bone marrow's ability to produce healthy red blood cells, iron deficiency can worsen anemia symptoms like fatigue, weakness, shortness of breath, and pale skin. Doctors commonly diagnose it through a complete blood count (CBC) and iron studies, which measure ferritin, transferrin saturation, and total iron-binding capacity.
