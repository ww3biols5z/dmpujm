Talk to your health care provider if you have persistent worsening back pain. You may need to seek care more urgently or even emergency services if the pain is accompanied by an inability to catch your breath, fevers/chills, difficultly walking or standing, or a loss of bowel or bladder control. While back pain is generally benign it can also be life-threatening. And it is certainly not comfortable. If you have concerns, you can start by using a free symptom checker to better understand your symptoms, then talk with your health care provider.
