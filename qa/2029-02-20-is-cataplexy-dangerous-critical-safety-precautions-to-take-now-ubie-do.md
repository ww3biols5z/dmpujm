Explanation

Cataplexy is a condition where a person suddenly loses muscle strength. This can happen when they feel strong emotions, like laughter or surprise. It is not considered dangerous on its own, but it can be risky in certain situations.

Precautions to Take:

[to Take Now](https://lumina-dermis.sunsimiaosd.com/)

Avoid Dangerous Activities: People with cataplexy should be careful when doing things where sudden weakness could cause harm, like driving a car or using heavy machines.
