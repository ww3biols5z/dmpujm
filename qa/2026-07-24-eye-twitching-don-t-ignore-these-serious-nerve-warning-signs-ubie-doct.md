# Eye Twitching? Don't Ignore These Serious Nerve Warning Signs | Ubie Doctor's Note

Published on: 7/21/2026

Could frequent eye twitching be a sign of an underlying nerve or vision problem?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Frequent eye twitching, also known as myokymia, is usually harmless and temporary. Common causes include stress, fatigue, caffeine, eye strain, and dry eyes. In rare cases, persistent twitching may signal an underlying nerve or vision issue, such as blepharospasm, hemifacial spasm, or a neurological condition.
