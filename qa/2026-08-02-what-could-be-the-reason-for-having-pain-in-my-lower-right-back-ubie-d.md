Lower right back pain can have different causes. One possible reason is a condition called L4 radiculopathy. This happens when a nerve in your lower spine is pinched or irritated. This can cause pain that spreads from your back down to your leg.

Another possible cause is a duodenal ulcer. This is a sore in your stomach that can sometimes cause pain in your back.

If you are having pain, it is important to talk to a doctor who can help find the exact cause and the best way to help you feel better.
