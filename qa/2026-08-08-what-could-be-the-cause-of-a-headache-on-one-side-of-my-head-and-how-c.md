Headaches on one side of the head can often be migraines. They can be treated with medications and lifestyle changes.

Explanation

Headaches on one side of the head are commonly migraines, which can cause severe pain and other symptoms. According to a review, migraines can occur on either the left or right side and might be linked to different triggers or factors.

Causes

Central and Peripheral Processes: Headaches can be caused by changes in the brain or nerves outside the brain.

Genetic and Biochemical Factors: Changes in brain chemicals, especially serotonin, may play a role in migraines.

Treatment Options

Medications: Pain relief medicines and those that reduce migraine frequency can help.

Lifestyle Changes: Avoiding known triggers, regular sleep, and stress management may reduce headaches.
