Can Daytime Stimulants Cause Sleepwalking?

Yes, daytime stimulant medications—such as those prescribed for ADHD—may influence nighttime sleep behaviors, including sleepwalking (somnambulism). This effect is more commonly observed in children, particularly those with underlying conditions like ADHD, anxiety, or existing sleep disorders. Stimulants can disrupt sleep architecture, delay sleep onset, and increase the likelihood of parasomnias in susceptible individuals.

If you or your child are experiencing sleepwalking or other unusual sleep behaviors, understanding the potential cause is the first step toward better rest and safer nights.
