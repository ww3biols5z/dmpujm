Sciatica pain can radiate to the front of the thigh, though this is less common than pain in the back of the leg. The sciatic nerve primarily affects the buttocks, back of the thigh, and lower leg, but nerve root irritation in the lower lumbar spine (L2–L4) can cause anterior thigh pain—a condition sometimes referred to as "femoral nerve" involvement rather than true sciatica.

Because front-of-thigh pain can stem from several causes—including nerve compression, hip issues, or muscle strain—identifying the source is key to effective treatment.
