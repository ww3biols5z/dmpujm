When Is Anxiety a Red Flag?

Anxiety can be unsettling, but it's not always dangerous. However, you should seek immediate medical attention if you experience:

Chest pain or tightness

Trouble breathing or shortness of breath

Severe dizziness or fainting

Extreme heart palpitations (over 120 bpm)

Thoughts of harming yourself
