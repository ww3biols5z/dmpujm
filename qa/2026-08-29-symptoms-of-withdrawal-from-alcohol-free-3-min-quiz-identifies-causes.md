About the Symptom

Alcohol withdrawal can produce symptoms such as shakiness, headache, irritability, heart palpitations, or insomnia. In more severe cases, alcohol withdrawal can cause seizures, visual hallucinations, confusion, agitation, and death.

When to See a Doctor

Seek professional care if you experience any of the following symptoms

Irritable or tremors after stopping alcohol
