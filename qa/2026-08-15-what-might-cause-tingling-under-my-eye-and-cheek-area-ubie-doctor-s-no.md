Explanation

Tingling sensations under the eye and cheek are usually related to nerves in the face, such as the infraorbital nerve, which supplies sensation to these areas. Common causes include:

Nerve irritation or pressure: This can happen from facial injury, dental procedures, or swelling from infections or allergies. Sinus issues: Inflammation or infection in the sinuses can put pressure on nearby nerves causing tingling or numbness. Skin conditions or allergies: Rashes or allergic reactions can cause tingling sensations in the facial skin. Other causes: Migraines, nerve disorders, or anxiety can also cause facial tingling. If the tingling is mild and short-lived, it often isn’t a cause for concern. However, if it lasts a long time, gets worse, or comes with other symptoms like numbness, weakness, or vision changes, it’s important to see a healthcare provider for evaluation.
