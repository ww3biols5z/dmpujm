Multiple myeloma does not typically cause balance problems directly. However, people with multiple myeloma may experience balance issues due to related complications, such as peripheral neuropathy from chemotherapy, anemia, spinal bone lesions, hypercalcemia, or medication side effects. These secondary factors—not the cancer itself—are the usual culprits behind unsteadiness or dizziness in myeloma patients.
