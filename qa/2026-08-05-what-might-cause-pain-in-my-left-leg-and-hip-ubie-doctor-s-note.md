Explanation

Pain in the left leg and hip can have several causes. One possible reason is advanced osteoarthritis of the hip, which can lead to significant discomfort and difficulty moving the joint. This condition involves the wearing down of the cartilage that cushions the hip joint, leading to pain and stiffness.

Another cause could be greater trochanter bursitis, especially in females. This condition involves inflammation of the bursa, a small fluid-filled sac near the hip joint, which can result in pain on the outside of the hip and thigh.

Lastly, deep venous thrombosis (DVT) may also cause pain in the leg. This is a serious condition where a blood clot forms in a deep vein, usually in the legs, leading to swelling, pain, and possibly more severe complications if not treated promptly.
