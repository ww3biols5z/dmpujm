Explanation

Infertility means having trouble getting pregnant after a year of trying. Common causes include problems with ovulation, blocked fallopian tubes, or issues with sperm.

Causes of Infertility:

Women:

Ovulation problems: This happens when eggs do not mature or release properly.

[common infertility](https://aegispeptide.snaketongueofficial.com/)

Blocked fallopian tubes: These tubes carry eggs from the ovaries to the uterus and can get blocked by infections or other conditions.

Men:

Sperm problems: This includes low sperm count or sperm that do not move well.

Treatments for Infertility:

Medications:

Medicines can help women ovulate.

Surgery:

[issues and their treatments](https://regeneration-desk.hbsnzj.com/blog/7214600940.html)

Surgery can fix blocked fallopian tubes or remove tissue that should not be there.

Assisted Reproductive Technologies (ART):

In Vitro Fertilization (IVF): This involves taking eggs and sperm out of the body, fertilizing them in a lab, and then putting them back in the uterus.

Finding the right treatment depends on the cause of infertility, so it's important to talk to a doctor to find the best plan.
