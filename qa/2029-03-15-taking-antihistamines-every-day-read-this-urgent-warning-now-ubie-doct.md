Is it okay to take antihistamines every day?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Yes, taking antihistamines daily is generally safe for most people, particularly second-generation antihistamines like cetirizine, loratadine, and fexofenadine. These newer options are non-drowsy and designed for long-term daily use to manage chronic allergies. First-generation antihistamines (such as diphenhydramine), however, are not recommended for daily use due to sedation and other side effects.

Daily vs. As-Needed Use

Some studies, like one comparing daily desloratadine use to as-needed use, suggest that taking antihistamines daily can improve quality of life for people with persistent allergy symptoms (Reference 1).

On-Demand Use

On-demand use of non-sedating antihistamines can also be effective for controlling symptoms, but daily use may provide more consistent relief (Reference 3).
