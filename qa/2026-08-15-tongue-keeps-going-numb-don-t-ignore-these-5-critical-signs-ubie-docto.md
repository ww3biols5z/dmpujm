Tongue numbness is often caused by nerve trauma from head injuries, dental procedures, or surgeries such as tonsillectomies. In rare cases, it may result from complications following throat or oral surgery. Other potential causes include allergic reactions, vitamin deficiencies (like B12), nerve disorders, or underlying neurological conditions such as multiple sclerosis or stroke.

Explanation

Tongue numbness can be concerning, and understanding possible causes is important.

Head Trauma: Sometimes, after a head injury, people might experience tongue paralysis or numbness. This happens because the nerves controlling the tongue can be affected by the trauma.
