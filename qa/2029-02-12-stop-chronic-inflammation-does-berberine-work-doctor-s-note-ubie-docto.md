Is berberine effective for reducing chronic inflammation?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Berberine, a plant compound found in herbs like goldenseal and barberry, may help reduce chronic inflammation. Research suggests berberine can lower inflammatory markers such as C-reactive protein (CRP) and interleukin-6 (IL-6), potentially benefiting conditions like type 2 diabetes, heart disease, and metabolic syndrome. However, more high-quality clinical studies are needed to fully confirm its effects and establish safe dosing.

If you're experiencing symptoms of chronic inflammation—such as fatigue, joint pain, digestive issues, or unexplained discomfort—it's important to understand the root cause before trying supplements like berberine.
