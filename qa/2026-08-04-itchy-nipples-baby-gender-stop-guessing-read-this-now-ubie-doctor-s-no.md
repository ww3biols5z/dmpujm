# Itchy Nipples & Baby Gender? Stop Guessing & Read This Now! | Ubie Doctor's Note

Published on: 7/21/2026

Is there any truth to the idea that itchy nipples during pregnancy indicate the baby's gender?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Itchy nipples during pregnancy are not a reliable predictor of your baby's gender. No scientific evidence supports the belief that nipple itching—or its intensity—can indicate whether you're carrying a boy or a girl. Itchy nipples are typically caused by hormonal changes, skin stretching, and increased blood flow to breast tissue, all common and expected during pregnancy.

While gender-prediction myths can be fun, they shouldn't guide health decisions. If itching becomes intense, persistent, or is paired with other symptoms, it could point to something worth investigating—like dermatitis, eczema, or, rarely, intrahepatic cholestasis of pregnancy.
