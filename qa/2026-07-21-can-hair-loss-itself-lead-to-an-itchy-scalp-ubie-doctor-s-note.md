Explanation

Hair loss does not directly lead to an itchy scalp. Instead, itchiness may be related to other conditions like a sensitive scalp. A sensitive scalp can cause discomfort and itchiness, and it is often influenced by external factors such as hair care practices.

Sensitive Scalp: A sensitive scalp can be more prone to itching. This condition can be aggravated by hair care products or environmental factors.

Hair Care Practices: The way people care for their hair can affect scalp sensitivity and potentially lead to itchiness.

Management: Managing a sensitive scalp involves identifying and avoiding triggers, such as harsh shampoos or hair treatments.

Understanding the relationship between these factors can help in managing scalp discomfort effectively. If you're experiencing persistent itchiness, it may be helpful to evaluate your hair care routine or consult a healthcare professional for further advice.
