Penile fracture is a rare but serious injury that can occur during sexual activity. It typically happens when there is a sudden, blunt trauma to an erect penis. To help prevent this, it is important to be cautious during sex.

Tips to Prevent Penile Fracture:

Avoid High-Risk Positions: Certain sexual positions may increase the risk of penile fracture because they can cause the penis to bend sharply or be subjected to excessive force. Choose positions that allow for more control and less risk of sudden impact.

Use Adequate Lubrication: Ensuring that there is enough lubrication can reduce friction and the chance of injury.

Communicate with Your Partner: Open communication with your partner can help ensure that both parties are comfortable and aware of movements that could cause injury.
