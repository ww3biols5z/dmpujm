Difficulty raising your arm above your shoulder can result from several conditions, including diabetes-related frozen shoulder (adhesive capsulitis), nerve damage following surgery, or complications after radiotherapy. Other common causes include rotator cuff injuries, tendonitis, or impingement syndrome. Because limited shoulder mobility can signal anything from a minor strain to a more serious nerve or joint issue, an accurate diagnosis is essential to guide treatment and prevent long-term stiffness or weakness.

Explanation

Having trouble lifting your arm away from your body can be caused by different health issues.

Diabetes: People with diabetes can develop shoulder problems, like a frozen shoulder. This might make it hard to move the shoulder and cause pain.

Nerve Injuries: After certain surgeries, like those on the neck, nerves can be affected. This might lead to weakness or trouble moving the arm.
