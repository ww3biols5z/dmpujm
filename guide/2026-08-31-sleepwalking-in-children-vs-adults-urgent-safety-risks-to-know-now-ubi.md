Is sleepwalking more common in children than adults?

Internal Medicine

Sleepwalking is significantly more common in children than in adults. Research shows that up to 15% of children experience sleepwalking episodes, while only about 1.5% to 4% of adults are affected. Most children naturally outgrow sleepwalking by adolescence as their nervous systems mature and sleep patterns stabilize.

If sleepwalking is disrupting your life or a loved one's, it's worth understanding what's driving it. Underlying factors like sleep deprivation, stress, fever, or certain medications can trigger episodes—and pinpointing the cause is the first step toward better sleep.
