Explanation

Period cramps can be uncomfortable, but there are ways to help manage them. Over-the-counter medications can provide relief from period pain and are commonly used for this purpose. These medications work by reducing inflammation (including reducing the production of prostaglandins, which are chemicals in the body that cause pain and inflammation during menstruation) and/or blocking pain signals.

In addition to medications, some people find relief using herbal and dietary supplements. While some supplements might help, the evidence is not strong enough to recommend them universally. It's important to note that more research is needed to fully understand how these supplements work and how effective and safe they are.

If you are considering using supplements, it might be a good idea to talk to a healthcare professional to ensure they are safe and appropriate for you. If your period pain is severe or unusual, you can use a free symptom checker to help understand your symptoms better. Remember, what works for one person might not work for another, so it might take some time to find what helps you best.
